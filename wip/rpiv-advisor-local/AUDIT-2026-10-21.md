# Advisor audit, 2026-10-21

The user asked whether the advisor is useful or only eats Claude usage, and whether its caching works.

**Cost.** 50 calls from 2026-09-30 to 10-20 (35 in find-gaps cycles, 15 in the pi-lab session): $61.74 API-equivalent, about $3/day, about 1 % of the day's Claude use ($270-390/day). Until 10-16 it ran on GPT (no cache figures); since 10-17 on `anthropic/claude-fable-5-1`.

**Caching works as designed.** Each request sends the caller's whole conversation (110-285k tokens). The advisor model (Fable) differs from the caller's (Opus), and Anthropic caches per model, so a first call is always a cold 5-minute cache write at 1.25x input. A second call within 5 minutes reads it at 0.1x (10-18 06:02: 225k read, $0.09). 1 of 9 Fable calls had such a follow-up. Turning caching off for one-shot calls would save about 12 % of the advisor's cost (~$0.4/day): not worth a patch.

**Usefulness** (a Sonnet Explore judged each call from the session's messages before and after; `/tmp/adv/calls.md`, not kept):

| Label | cycles (35) | pi-lab (15) |
|---|---|---|
| Changed what the session did | 9 | 6 |
| Small concrete addition | 15 | 7 |
| Only confirmed the plan | 7 | 1 |
| Wrong or ignored | 2 | 0 |
| Failed (API error, usage limit) | 2 | 1 |

Most valuable: builder briefs that broke the user's recorded rules before paid bulk work (2 calls), real bugs in live extensions (`arg-fixes` escapes, a control-character guard bypass, `bg-bash` ordering), and "verified"/"deployed" claims without evidence. Noise: "done / proceed" confirmations at cycle end (~$2.5-3.3 each), one duplicate call, one empty echo. The same pre-"done" call slot also caught real errors (a wrong "deployed" claim), so the noise cannot be cut by rule without losing those.

**Decision:** keep it unchanged. 15 changed outcomes for ~$62 is cheap next to one bad deploy or one paid batch run against the user's rules.
